CV Download Files

To use actual PDF files for your CVs:
1. Replace the placeholder files with your actual CV PDFs
2. Name them as:
   - Jdey-Iheb-CV-English.pdf (for English version)
   - Jdey-Iheb-CV-French.pdf (for French version)
3. Place them in the /public/cv/ directory

The download links in the portfolio will work automatically once the actual PDF files are in place.
